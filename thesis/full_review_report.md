## Cross-cutting observations

- **The product-aggregation claim is inconsistent across the thesis and mathematically wrong in the Methodology (W-02, K-03).** §3.4 rejects the normalised product for permitting single-task specialisation; §5.6 recommends it as the cure for exactly that problem. Both cannot stand, and the Methodology's version is the incorrect one.
- **NEAT's complexification is described as fitness-conditioned structure addition in three separate places (W-01a/b/c).** This misstates the algorithm the whole thesis rests on and undercuts the very motivation for speciation that the same paragraphs give.
- **The Introduction's confidence and the Results' statistics disagree (C-01).** The Introduction promises that the design makes an absence of difference "statistically meaningful"; §5.2 correctly reports that only effects of $d \geq 0.74$ can be ruled out. The Results are right; the Introduction should be brought down to them.
- **The thesis has no abstract and an empty Conclusion, and RQ1 is never answered (T-01, T-02, K-06).** The document currently ends mid-analysis: limitations and future work are stranded inside the Discussion, and the reader is never told whether a single genome *did* learn both tasks.
